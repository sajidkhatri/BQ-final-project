Emergency Contacts

Below are the most important numbers to keep in mind during an emergency. Save these to your phone now, so you're not searching for them under pressure later.

If you're unsure which service to call, start with Police or Ambulance — they can redirect you to the right department if needed.

Primary Numbers

Police

15

For crimes, threats, accidents, or public safety concerns.

Ambulance

1122

For medical emergencies, injuries, or when someone needs urgent transport to a hospital.

Fire Brigade

16

For fires, gas leaks, or hazardous material incidents.

Poison Control

111-222-333

For accidental poisoning, overdoses, or chemical exposure.

Gas Complaint

1199

For gas leaks or gas supply emergencies.

Electricity Complaint

118

For power faults, downed lines, or electric shock hazards.

What to Say When You Call

  • State the type of emergency clearly (medical, fire, crime, etc.)
  • Give your exact location, including landmarks if possible
  • Describe the number of people involved and their condition
  • Stay on the line until the operator tells you it's okay to hang up
